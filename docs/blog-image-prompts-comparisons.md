# Blog image prompts (7 comparison SEO posts)

Drop finished PNGs into `public/assests/blogs/` using the **exact filenames** below. Paths are already wired in the posts. Do not add watermarks, extra brand logos, fake QR Junction UI chrome, trademarked competitor marks (no Monkey / TIGER / Bitly / Canva logos), or unreadable lorem text.

**Global rules for every image**

- Photorealistic, natural light, India-relevant settings unless the shot is a product diagram.
- QR patterns must look like real scannable codes (sharp modules, quiet zone, three finder squares). Do not smear them into abstract noise.
- No stock-photo “fake laptop dashboards” with illegible Latin gibberish. If UI text is visible, keep it short and in English: Menu, Scans, WiFi, WhatsApp, SVG, PNG, Static, Dynamic, URL.
- No celebrity faces, no readable personal phone numbers, no real UPI IDs.
- Colour grade: warm, slightly editorial, not neon cyberpunk.
- Export PNG, sRGB, unsharpened.

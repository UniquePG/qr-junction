Your direction is useful, but there is a trap: **if QR Junction becomes “menus with lots of animations,” it will look gimmicky rather than premium.** The differentiation should come from making the menu feel like a **digital experience built around the restaurant**, while still making ordering faster.

Your strongest idea is not “Instagram/Pinterest menu.” It is:

> **Let a business choose an experience style for its menu, rather than forcing every business into the same grid/card template.**

That can become a real QR Junction product category.

# 23. The Bigger Architecture

I wouldn't build these as separate random templates.

Build QR Junction around **four layers**:

```text
                QR JUNCTION MENU
                       │
          ┌────────────┴────────────┐
          │                         │
     EXPERIENCE                  BUSINESS
          │                         │
   Layout Engine              Analytics
   Theme Engine               Upselling
   Animation Engine           Offers
   Personality Engine         Conversion
          │                    Customer data
          │
      INTERACTION
          │
   Recommendations
   AI Discovery
   Smart Cart
   Loyalty
   Reviews
   Service Requests
```

Then you can combine them.

For example:

### Restaurant A

**Luxury + Editorial + Chef Mode**

### Restaurant B

**Street + Social Feed + Playful**

### Restaurant C

**Diwali + Bento + Desi Personality**

### Restaurant D

**Minimal + AI Discovery + Premium**

Same underlying QR Junction system.

Completely different experiences.

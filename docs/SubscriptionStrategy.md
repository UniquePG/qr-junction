The weak point in your current idea is **giving 2–3 months free to everyone**. That can get adoption, but it also teaches the restaurant owner that QR Junction is a free utility. When you finally ask for money, you are changing the customer's mental price from ₹0 to ₹299/₹499.

You should use **monthly SaaS as the core model**, but use long free periods only as a controlled **founding-customer acquisition strategy**.

I checked your QR Junction product direction and the current Indian QR-menu market. Your existing product is actually broader than a basic QR-menu tool: you already have dynamic QR infrastructure, scan analytics, leads, reviews, restaurant menus, table QR, AI menu import, etc. Your roadmap also explicitly targets MRR, subscriptions, agency and enterprise plans. So I would **not position QR Junction as merely "a QR menu for ₹99."** That would leave a lot of your product value unmonetized.
